    Screen stripTouch screen with the recipe bar, a camera that reads recipe cards, a mic for her voice and questions, and a speaker.

    Guidance surfaceDraws outlines, cut lines at real size, and step numbers. Ink line drawing only, like a printed instruction sheet.

    Scale in the four feetLoad cells weigh anything on the board and check ingredients off by weight. Target: 1 g steps up to 5 kg.
  4. 4
    Removable chopping insertKnives never touch the glass. The insert lifts out to wash.

Open questions before building

  • How do the lines show through a chopping insert? Options: a clear insert over the display, projection from the screen strip, or chopping only on one side.
  • Display under glass or projection? A display costs more and adds weight. Projection needs a mast and gets washed out in a bright kitchen.
  • Water, heat and knife marks: what does food-safe and dishwasher-safe mean for each layer?
  • Mains power or battery, and how long does a 3-hour rendang need it to last?
