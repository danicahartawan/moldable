# Moldable kitchen mockup

The display on a wooden kitchen table, with the Home and Explore wireframes live on its screen. Click and swipe on the screen; the switch underneath changes screens.

- `index.html`: the whole mockup (3D scene, screen logic and both wireframes)
- `assets/lelawar.jpg`: the dish photo used on the screens

## Opening it
Double-click `index.html`, or serve the folder (for example `npx serve .`) if your browser blocks local files. It needs an internet connection: three.js, Preact/htm and the Google fonts load from CDNs.

## Notes
- Fonts are DM Sans and Fraunces, standing in for ABC Arizona (trial licence, not redistributable).
- The wireframes are copies of the canvas boards as of 18 Oct 2026; later canvas edits aren't included.
- The device is a generic smart display with no Apple branding.
- Photo rights: the dish photo was supplied as a reference; check you can use it before sharing.
